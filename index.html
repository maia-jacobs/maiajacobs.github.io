About

Maia Jacobs is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science and Preventive Medicine at Northwestern University, where she holds the Lisa Wissner-Slivka and Benjamin Slivka Chair in Computer Science and directs the NU-PATH Lab (Personalized and Adaptive Technology for Health). Her work sits at the intersection of human-computer interaction, machine learning, and health — designing and evaluating technologies that support people's health needs as they change over time, from clinical decision-support tools built with patients and clinicians to community-driven mental health interventions.

She earned her PhD in Human-Centered Computing from the Georgia Institute of Technology and completed a postdoctoral fellowship at Harvard's Center for Research on Computation and Society before joining Northwestern in 2021.

Research focus

Human-Computer Interaction AI in Healthcare Digital Mental Health Personalized Health Technology Community-Engaged Design

The NU-PATH Lab uses HCI methods to design and evaluate technologies that support people's dynamic health needs and goals — spanning personalized tools for long-term health management, interfaces for ML-driven clinical decision support, and co-designed digital solutions for community mental health.
