Research

How people understand, trust, and work with AI in high-stakes decisions — and how interface design reshapes judgment, expertise, and responsibility.

Overview

The NU-PATH Lab studies human-AI interaction in consequential, high-impact settings. Healthcare — where predictive models and AI recommendations are increasingly woven into clinical judgment — is the lab's primary testbed, but the underlying questions apply to high-stakes decision-making more broadly. The lab's work spans three connected themes:

  • AI-assisted decision-making — designing how people interact with AI recommendations and predictions in real, high-stakes, time-constrained settings, such as clinicians weighing an ML-driven risk score.
  • Trust, expertise, and invisible labor — understanding how AI changes what expertise looks like, what new work it creates for the people using it, and how interfaces can support rather than override human judgment.
  • Community-centered AI design — co-designing AI-based tools directly with the people they're meant to serve, from frontline workers to patients and families, so systems reflect real needs and context.

Current projects

Examples of these themes in the lab's current work:

Health-AI Decision Support Tools

Examines how healthcare AI systems often fail to account for the socio-technical complexities present in real clinical workflows.

CASMI: Co-designing Patient-Facing Machine Learning for Prenatal Stress Reduction

Includes patients directly in the development and design of machine-learning prediction tools for prenatal stress.

Co-designing Interventions for Burnout in Frontline Gender-Based Violence Work

A community-based participatory project co-designing interventions with frontline workers at nonprofit gender-based-violence survivor service organizations.

EquityWare: Designing Equitable Wearables

Democratizing wearable technologies by developing and co-designing inclusive wearables with and for marginalized groups.

Understanding Self-Reflection Practices

Researching scalable, effective interventions in response to growing mental health concerns.

Digital Mental Health Interventions for Youth Learning CS

Designing strategies and interventions that support youth's mental health within computer science education environments.

Team science
